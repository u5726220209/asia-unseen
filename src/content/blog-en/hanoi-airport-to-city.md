**The cheapest way into Hanoi costs 12,000 VND, the most expensive shuttle around 90,000.** That is a factor of seven for the same journey, and the difference is mostly a matter of knowing which desk to walk past.

Everything below is taken from the airport operator's own published list.

## The buses, cheapest to most direct

| Route | Journey | Fare | Hours |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 07 | Noi Bai – Cau Giay | 12,000 VND | 05:00 – 22:30 |
| 17 | Noi Bai – Long Bien | 15,000 VND | 05:10 – 22:00 |
| 90 | Noi Bai – Kim Ma | 15,000 VND | 06:40 – 22:30 |
| 109 | Noi Bai – My Dinh | 12,000 VND | 05:00 – 22:00 |
| E10 | Noi Bai – Ocean Park | 20,000 VND | 06:20 – 22:00 |

These are the ordinary city routes. They are cheap, they run often, and they stop where locals need them — which is rarely where you are staying. Expect a second leg by taxi or ride-hailing at the far end.

The two “quality” routes are more direct and considerably more comfortable:

| Route | Journey | Fare |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 86 | Noi Bai – Hanoi railway station | 50,000 VND |
| 68 | Noi Bai – Ha Dong | 55,000 VND |

**Route 86 is the one to remember.** It ends at Hanoi railway station, a short ride from the old quarter, runs every 25 to 30 minutes from 07:00 to 22:00, and takes luggage without fuss. At 50,000 VND it is still roughly a tenth of a taxi.

## Taxis: a regulated framework since 2024

Since July 2024, **only taxi companies accredited by the airport may wait and pick up at the terminals**, with sealed and calibrated meters.

That changes where the risk sits. It is no longer in the official rank; it is with the people who approach you inside the hall, before you reach it, quoting a fixed price that appears on no published list.

What to do, concretely: **walk out of the arrivals hall and join the marked rank.** Decline anyone who comes to you first, however official the badge looks. This is the same mechanism described in [our airport taxi guide](/en/blog/taxi-from-the-airport) for the rest of Asia: the scam only works where no tariff is published, and here one is.

## What does not exist yet

**There is no train or metro to Noi Bai.** Hanoi has metro lines; none of them reaches the airport. Anyone offering you an “airport express train” is selling something else.

## What to remember

- **12,000 to 20,000 VND** on ordinary buses, **50,000** on route 86 to the railway station.
- **Taxis are accredited** at the terminals since 2024 — use the marked rank, not the person who approaches you.
- **No train, no metro.** Bus or taxi, nothing else.
