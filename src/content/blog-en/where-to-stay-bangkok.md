**In Bangkok, two kilometres can take forty minutes.** That single fact decides where to sleep, and it is why the usual advice — pick a district by its character — gets people into trouble.

The real question is: how many minutes on foot from which station?

## The four areas that work

### Sukhumvit, between Asok and Thong Lo

**Who it suits:** a first visit, business trips, families. It is the default choice, and for once the default is right.

Dense in restaurants, served by both the BTS and the MRT, priced across every bracket from hostel to five-star.

**The trade-off:** it is the least Thai part of the city. Some *sois* are frankly given over to sex tourism — look at the lane number before you book.

### Silom and Sathorn

**Who it suits:** people who want to be central without Sukhumvit's intensity, and stays that include an evening on the rooftop bars.

Business by day, lively by night, and well connected on both networks.

### The riverside and the historic quarter

**Who it suits:** short, temple-focused stays, and travellers who want to see the city from the water.

**What to check:** the historic quarter has no BTS station. MRT Sanam Chai now serves it, and the Chao Phraya express boats do the rest. Make sure your hotel is within ten minutes of one or the other — beyond that, you depend on traffic.

### Ari

**Who it suits:** a second visit, remote work, or anyone who finds Sukhumvit tiring. Fifteen BTS minutes from the centre, with larger rooms at the same price.

## The area that divides opinion: Khao San

**The objection is logistical, not moral.** It is the only major tourist area in Bangkok **with neither a BTS nor an MRT station**. Every trip out means a taxi, a boat or a long walk. Across three nights that is several hours gone.

If you want the atmosphere — and it is genuinely worth one evening — go for the evening and sleep somewhere connected.

## One thing to sort out before you book

How long you may stay, which is not the same for everyone. The Thai exemption scheme changed on 15 September 2026, and the length now depends on your passport and on the date you enter the country, not on the date you booked.

The [Thailand page](/en/thailande) gives the rule for yours, with the government source it came from and the month it was read. Worth two minutes before you commit to a non-refundable booking of more than a month.

## What to remember

- **Ten minutes on foot from a BTS or MRT station.** That criterion outranks every district's reputation.
- **Sukhumvit for a first visit**, Ari for a second, the river for temples.
- **Khao San for an evening**, not for a bed.
